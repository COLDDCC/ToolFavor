What does “free AI logo generator” really mean?

Free to generate ≠ free to download. Some services allow previews but charge for the final file. A free PNG does not imply a free SVG vector file, a commercial license, or trademark exclusivity.

We separate prompt-based image generation from AI-assisted and template-based logo editors. A template customizer can still be useful, but it is not the same as generating a logo from a text prompt.

Frequently asked questions

Which free AI logo maker offers SVG downloads?

Namecheap's official logo maker page advertises free SVG and PNG downloads. Confirm its current sign-in requirements before starting.

Can I download a logo for free without an account?

FreeLogo.app advertises no-account PNG downloads; this is a vendor claim rather than an independently completed export test. Other services may require an account, email or a specific export flow.

Are Canva and Shopify true text-to-image AI logo generators?

They offer useful logo creation workflows, but their editors and templates are not equivalent to every prompt-based image generation feature. We label the approaches separately.

Can I trademark a logo made with a free generator?

Free access does not guarantee exclusive rights or trademark eligibility. Check the provider's asset licenses and perform a trademark search before commercial adoption.
