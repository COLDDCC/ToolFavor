How to choose the best free AI video generator

First decide whether you need text-to-video (generate a scene from a written prompt) or image-to-video (animate a still image). For social content made from existing footage, an AI-assisted editor may be more useful than a generative model.

  1. Compare credits: Daily and monthly refreshes are more useful for ongoing work than one-time signup credits.
  2. Compare exports: Look for watermarks, short clip limits, resolution caps and queues.
  3. Check rights: A free trial does not automatically permit commercial use.
  4. Test the same prompt: Compare motion consistency and how many retries each service needs.

Text-to-video vs image-to-video

Use text-to-video when you want to explore new scenes. Use image-to-video when you already have a reference photo or a consistent character or product to animate. Remember that the model shown in a provider's promotional examples may not be available on its free plan.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a completely free AI video generator?

Some tools offer limited recurring credits, but unlimited high-quality generation and unrestricted exports are uncommon.

Can I create free AI videos without a watermark?

Sometimes, but watermark rules depend on the tool and model. Always check the exact free export terms.

Can I generate AI videos without signing up?

Most major hosted generators require an account. Free access does not necessarily mean no registration.

Can I use free AI videos commercially?

Not automatically. Review each provider’s terms; some free tiers are for personal use only.

Which is better: text-to-video or image-to-video?

Text-to-video is useful for exploring ideas; image-to-video offers more control when you already have a reference image.
