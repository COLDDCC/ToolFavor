Who are the Duolingo characters?

These are well-known recurring characters, not an exhaustive list of every character or temporary mascot appearance.

Mascot

Duo

The green owl mascot associated with language-learning reminders and streaks.

Student

Lily

A dry-humored, introverted character, often paired with her outgoing best friend Zari.

Student

Zari

An enthusiastic, high-achieving student and Lily's close friend.

Writer & traveler

Bea

Ambitious and highly organized; one of the recurring story characters.

Free spirit

Lin

A laid-back character; Lucy is her grandmother.

Grandmother

Lucy

Lin's grandmother, known for her intriguing past.

Art teacher

Oscar

An art teacher with a distinctive mustache and an interest in culture.

Father

Eddy

Junior's father and an energetic, fitness-loving recurring character.

Child

Junior

Eddy's son, whose mischief often drives story situations.

Recurring character

Vikram

Appears in language lessons and examples across Duolingo content.

Bear

Falstaff

The bear character who appears in lessons, speaking exercises and notifications.

Character names and relationships

Lily and Zari: Best friends with contrasting personalities. Lin and Lucy: Granddaughter and grandmother. Eddy and Junior: Father and son. These relationships are described in Duolingo's own editorial content.

Some unofficial lists mix older designs, background characters and fan theories. For reliable character details, prefer Duolingo's official blog over unsourced speculation.

Are Duolingo characters AI?

Duolingo's characters are designed fictional personalities. Some Duolingo learning experiences use technology such as text-to-speech and AI-powered roleplay or video conversations. That does not mean every character illustration or storyline is AI-generated. For example, Duolingo has described how character traits guide its AI roleplay experiences.
