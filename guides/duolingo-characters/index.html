All 11 Duolingo characters: names and personalities

Duolingo’s recurring cast includes the owl mascot Duo, students Lily and Zari, family members Eddy and Junior, Lin and Lucy, and other memorable characters. Use the gallery above to identify a face, then read the short profiles below.

These are well-known recurring characters, not an exhaustive list of every character or temporary mascot appearance.

Mascot

Duo

Duo is the green owl and the most recognizable face of Duolingo. Unlike the human story cast, Duo represents the app itself and appears in reminders, marketing and lessons.

Student

Lily

Lily is recognizable by her purple hair, understated expressions and dry humor. Her quiet, sarcastic personality contrasts with Zari’s energy; the two are best friends.

Student

Zari

Zari is an outgoing, enthusiastic student who often appears alongside Lily. Their contrasting personalities are a recurring part of Duolingo's character storytelling.

Writer & traveler

Bea

Bea is ambitious, energetic and organized. She is part of the recurring human cast used to make language-learning stories feel more personal.

Free spirit

Lin

Lin is a relaxed, independent character and Lucy’s granddaughter. Their family connection is useful when identifying characters across Duolingo stories.

Grandmother

Lucy

Lucy is Lin’s grandmother. Her adventurous backstory and family relationship make her distinct from the younger recurring characters.

Art teacher

Oscar

Oscar is an art teacher known for his dramatic, expressive personality. His recognizable mustache and distinctive delivery help distinguish him in speaking exercises.

Father

Eddy

Eddy is Junior’s father and is often associated with fitness and exercise. Their father-and-son dynamic appears in the learning stories.

Child

Junior

Junior is Eddy’s young son. His childlike perspective and interactions with his father help make family conversations more natural.

Recurring character

Vikram

Vikram is a friendly recurring character associated with hobbies such as gardening and baking. He is part of the broader cast beyond the best-known students.

Bear

Falstaff

Falstaff is the bear character, separate from Duo the owl. He is one of Duolingo’s recognizable animal characters.

Are Duolingo characters AI?

Duolingo's characters are designed fictional personalities. Some Duolingo learning experiences use technology such as text-to-speech and AI-powered roleplay or video conversations. That does not mean every character illustration or storyline is AI-generated. For example, Duolingo has described how character traits guide its AI roleplay experiences.
