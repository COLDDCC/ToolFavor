Which should you choose?

Want a focused overview first? Read our DeepSwapper review, including free photo and paid video limitations →

Choose DeepSwapper if you want to try swapping faces in still photos without signing up and value its advertised unlimited image swaps. Do not assume the video tool is free.

Try DeepSwapFace if a published allowance of up to ten free swaps per day fits your needs or you want to explore its GIF and video features. Its marketing says these are free, but check the actual export screen before relying on them.

For either service: use images you own or have permission to edit. Do not use face swaps to impersonate someone, deceive viewers or create intimate imagery without consent.

How to try each service

  1. Open the official website from the links above, not a similarly named lookalike domain.
  2. Choose the image swap workflow and upload an image you have permission to use.
  3. Choose a source face with good lighting and a clearly visible face.
  4. Review the result and confirm whether export is free and watermark-free.
  5. If you need video, check the current subscription or credit screen before uploading footage.

Privacy reminder: facial images can be sensitive. Read each provider’s current privacy policy before uploading photos of other people, children, or private content. The deletion statements above are claims made by the providers, not independently audited.

Frequently asked questions

Is DeepSwapper completely free?

Its homepage advertises unlimited free image face swaps. Its FAQ states that video face swapping requires a subscription. So “free” does not apply to every feature.

Is DeepSwapFace the same company as DeepSwapper?

They use different domains and publish different free-access terms. We have not verified common ownership, so do not assume they are the same service.

Which service offers free video face swapping?

DeepSwapper says its video feature needs a subscription. DeepSwapFace markets video face swapping as free, but specific free export conditions need account-level verification.

Which service has no watermark?

Both providers advertise watermark-free results. ToolFavor has not independently checked exports on every plan.

Do either of these tools store uploaded faces?

DeepSwapper says it does not store uploads unless save-history or saved-face settings are enabled. DeepSwapFace says it deletes photos within two hours. Read the latest privacy terms for details.
