Excalidraw

A sketch-style whiteboard for thinking out loud. Turn rough ideas into flowcharts, wireframes, architecture sketches and diagrams without making everything look like a polished slide.

What can you make?

↗ Flowcharts

Map a process, a decision tree or a user journey.

▤ Wireframes

Sketch screen layouts before committing to UI design.

◇ Architecture diagrams

Explain how services, data and components connect.

✎ Visual notes

Make quick concept maps for meetings or study.

Why use Excalidraw?

Drawing stays intentionally informal

The hand-drawn look signals that ideas are still flexible — useful for early discussion and feedback.

Start immediately in the browser

Open the canvas and begin sketching. Basic drawing does not require creating an account.

Share and export

Use the editor for collaborative sketches and export diagrams as images or editable Excalidraw files.

Where it is less suitable

For pixel-perfect UI mockups, complex vector illustration or full design systems, a precision-oriented design app may be a better fit. Excalidraw shines when speed and clarity matter more than exact alignment.
