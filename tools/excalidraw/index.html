Quick answers.

Can I use Excalidraw without signing up?

Yes. You can open the basic canvas and begin drawing without an account. Additional hosted services may have separate requirements.

Is Excalidraw completely free?

The core browser editor is free to use; paid offerings and hosted collaboration services may have their own plans.

Can I export a diagram?

Yes. The project supports image export and its own editable drawing format, so your work is not limited to a screenshot.

Is it better than Figma?

They serve different stages. Excalidraw is excellent for informal sketches and diagrams; Figma is better suited to precise interface layouts and polished design systems.
