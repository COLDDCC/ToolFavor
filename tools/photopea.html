MAKE A DECISION

Is Photopea right for you?

Good fit if…

  • You need to open or tweak a PSD without owning Photoshop
  • You're on a shared, school or work computer where you can't install software
  • You want layers and masks, not just one-tap filters
  • You'd rather your images stay on your own device

Compare first if…

  • You rely on Photoshop-only plugins or a full Adobe workflow
  • You need team collaboration and shared asset libraries
  • You only want quick template graphics — a template tool is faster
  • Ads take up 5–15% of the screen on the free version

QUICK ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions

Is Photopea free?

Yes. Photopea is free to use and is funded mainly by ads. Premium is an optional paid account; it includes a monthly allowance of AI credits. Check the official account page for the current price.

Check source ↗
Can Photopea open and save PSD files?

Yes. PSD is Photopea's main format and you can save back to PSD with layers intact. For very complex files, check the result before relying on it.

Check source ↗
Do I need to install anything?

No. It runs in the browser. You can optionally install it as a browser app from the More menu, but it still runs inside your browser.

Check source ↗
Does Photopea upload my images?

Photopea states that files you open are processed on your device and are not sent to its servers. If you connect cloud storage such as Google Drive, files travel between that service and your browser.

Check source ↗
Does Photopea have AI tools?

Yes — Photopea documents AI features such as background removal and Magic Replace. Premium includes a monthly AI credit allowance.

Check source ↗
