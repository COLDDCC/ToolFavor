What is DeepSwapper?

DeepSwapper is a web-based AI face-swapping tool for replacing faces in images. Its website also describes video face swaps. The similarly named Deepswap.ai and DeepSwapFace use different domains; check the address before paying or uploading.

What's free, and what costs money?

Photo face swaps

The official site advertises free image face swaps, unlimited usage, no registration and no watermarks. We have not independently verified every download or region.

Video face swaps

The provider's video FAQ says a subscription is needed. We do not publish an unverified price or promise a free video export.

Bottom line: “Free DeepSwapper” is a reasonable description of its advertised photo workflow, but not of all video features.

How to use DeepSwapper for photos

  1. Open the official website.
  2. Choose its photo face swap tool.
  3. Upload a target image and the face image you have permission to use.
  4. Generate the result and review the output before sharing it.
  5. Check the download screen for any changed limits or account requirements.

These are general workflow steps, not a recorded hands-on walkthrough. Upload only photos you own or have permission to edit.

Privacy and responsible use

DeepSwapper's published FAQ describes a default policy of not storing uploaded images, with exceptions for optional history or saved-face features. This is the provider's statement, not an independent privacy audit. Read the current terms before uploading sensitive photos or pictures of other people.

Never use face swaps to impersonate someone, mislead others or create non-consensual intimate content.

DeepSwapper alternatives

DeepSwapFace

DeepSwapFace advertises up to 10 free swaps per day and markets photo, video and GIF swapping. The exact video export conditions have not been independently verified.

Frequently asked questions

Is DeepSwapper free without an account?

For photo face swapping, its website advertises free use without registration. Video face swapping requires a subscription according to its FAQ.

Is DeepSwapper the same as Deepswap.ai?

No assumption should be made based on the similar names. DeepSwapper's website is deepswapper.com; Deepswap.ai operates at deepswap.ai.

Does DeepSwapper add a watermark?

Its website advertises watermark-free image output, but ToolFavor has not independently verified exports on every workflow.

Can I use DeepSwapper for videos for free?

Its official video FAQ says a subscription is required. Check its current checkout page for exact pricing.

Which is better, DeepSwapper or DeepSwapFace?

It depends on your usage: DeepSwapper advertises unlimited free photo swaps, while DeepSwapFace advertises a limited daily allowance. See the detailed comparison.
