Questions, answered

Before you try it.

Is DeepSwapper completely free?

Not for every workflow. The official site advertises free, unlimited image face swaps. Its video face-swap FAQ says a subscription is required for video swapping.

Can I use it without registering?

The provider advertises photo swapping without login. Account and payment requirements may differ for videos, saved history and other features.

Are DeepSwapper and Deepswap.ai the same?

Do not treat similar names as the same provider. This page covers the service at deepswapper.com.

Does it support video and GIF uploads?

The official site describes image, video and GIF workflows. Video swapping requires a subscription according to its video FAQ. Confirm the exact upload and export options in your current workflow.

Will the service keep my uploaded images?

The provider's FAQ describes storage exceptions for optional saved history or saved face settings. This is a vendor statement, not an independent security audit. Read its current privacy policy and settings.

Can I use swapped content commercially?

The provider discusses commercial uses, but rights to the original images, likeness consent, platform rules and local laws still matter. Check the current service terms before publishing commercial work.
